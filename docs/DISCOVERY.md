# Discovery

Discovery turns a raw idea into a product brief that is specific enough to build. This file is a working checklist, not marketing copy.

## Discovery Gate

Do not move to implementation until these are answered:

- Who is the primary audience?
- What painful problem or desire does the site address?
- What is the primary offer?
- What action should the visitor take?
- Why should the visitor trust the site?
- Which pages are required for version 1?
- What content or assets already exist?
- What must not be shown, promised, or implied?
- What makes the first release successful?

## Audience Detail

For each important audience:

| Audience | Need | Objection | Desired Proof | CTA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Small business / shop owner | website, landing page, sales funnel, digital setup | unclear price, quality, delivery time | portfolio, packages, process, support | request consultation |
| Individual digital buyer | buy tool, template, code, account, course | scam risk, inactive code, no support | warranty, delivery policy, reviews, clear terms | browse products |
| Learner / creator | learn digital skills or use digital templates | unsure about course value | outcomes, preview lessons, instructor proof | view courses |
| Reseller / partner | source digital goods or request bundles | reliability and margin | source policy, support response, terms | contact partnership |

## Brief Readiness Checklist

`docs/PRODUCT_BRIEF.md` is ready when:

- the primary audience is one sentence, not a vague market
- the offer is concrete enough to write page copy
- the primary CTA is chosen
- required pages are listed
- tone and visual direction are named
- SEO target topics are listed
- unknowns are marked as explicit assumptions

## Discovery Prompt

```text
Interview me to complete docs/DISCOVERY.md and docs/PRODUCT_BRIEF.md. Ask only high-impact questions that change the website strategy, page structure, content, CTA, SEO, visual direction, or deployment plan. Do not code. After the interview, summarize assumptions and blockers.
```
